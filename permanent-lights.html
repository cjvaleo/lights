Permanent lights or seasonal Christmas lights?

If you only want lights in December, our Christmas light installation is the simpler pick: we hang them, then take them down in January. If you'd use lights for more than one holiday, permanent lights make more sense. Read the full comparison: Permanent lights vs. Christmas lights.
