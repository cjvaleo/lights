Permanent lights vs. Christmas lights: which is right for your house?

Both get you a lit-up house with no ladder. The difference is whether you want lights for one season or every season.

By TR Lights, Toms River, NJ · Updated November 6, 2026

Short answer: Pick seasonal Christmas light installation if you only want lights in December. Pick permanent lights if you'd use them for more than one holiday, like the 4th, Halloween or game day.

Seasonal Christmas light installation

The installer hangs lights for the season and takes them down after the holidays.

  • Best for: classic Christmas looks, one season a year
  • Lights: with TR Lights, commercial-grade LED lights are included and cut to fit your roofline
  • Up and down: installed in November or December, taken down in January

Permanent lights

Lights mount under your roofline and stay up all year. You control them from an app.

  • Best for: people who decorate for several holidays, or want one install and done
  • Lights: TR Lights installs Govee permanent lights
  • Control: any color or effect from your phone, any night

Side by side

  • Holidays covered: Christmas only vs. every holiday
  • Ladders: none for you either way
  • Look in daytime: seasonal lights are visible all season; permanent lights tuck under the roofline and are hard to spot
  • Colors: set at install vs. change anytime from your phone

How to choose

Ask yourself one question: would you use colored lights outside of December? If yes, permanent lights. If no, Christmas light installation. Not sure? Text us a photo of your house and we'll price both side by side.

Questions

Are permanent lights better than Christmas lights?

Neither is better for everyone. Permanent lights fit people who want lights for many holidays. Seasonal installation fits people who only want lights in December.
