# What to ask before hiring a Christmas light installer

Six questions that separate a pro installer from a guy with a ladder.

> **Short answer:** Ask whether they're insured, whether lights are included, whether they cut lights to fit your house, whether they do 2 and 3-story roofs, whether takedown is included, and how they quote.

## 1. Are you insured?

Your installer is on your roof. Ask for proof of liability insurance. TR Lights is fully insured with commercial general liability coverage.

## 2. Are the lights included?

Some installers make you buy and supply the lights yourself. Others include them. With TR Lights, commercial-grade LED lights are included, so there's nothing to buy.

## 3. Do you cut the lights to fit my house?

Off-the-spool lights leave gaps or extra strands hanging. Ask whether they measure and cut to your roofline. TR Lights cuts every light to fit.

## 4. Can you do my roof?

Two and three-story homes and steep peaks take the right gear and experience. TR Lights installs on 1, 2 and 3-story homes, steep roofs included.

## 5. Is takedown included?

Find out who takes the lights down in January, and whether it costs extra. TR Lights includes takedown.

## 6. How do you quote?

Every house is different, so most pros price per house. TR Lights looks up your address and texts you a price, usually the same day. [Get your free quote](/#quote).
