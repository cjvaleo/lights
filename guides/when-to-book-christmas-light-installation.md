# When to book Christmas light installation in New Jersey

Book in October or early November to get the install date you want. December dates go first.

> **Short answer:** Book Christmas light installation by early November. Installers in Ocean and Monmouth County fill their calendars first come, first served, and the first two weeks of December go fastest.

## Why booking early matters

Christmas light installers only have a few weeks to hang every job. A small crew can only do so many houses a day, so once the calendar is full, it's full. Booking early gets you:

- **Your pick of install dates,** including lights up before Thanksgiving weekend
- **Early-booking deals,** which most installers offer before mid-November
- **No scramble** if you're hosting or want photos with the lights on

## When do installs happen?

Most installs run from early November through mid-December. Lights go up with a timer so they turn on by themselves each night.

## When do the lights come down?

After the holidays, in January. With TR Lights, takedown is included and scheduled when you book.

## Want lights you never have to book again?

[Permanent lights](/permanent-lights) stay up all year. You turn them on for Christmas, then switch colors for every other holiday from your phone.
